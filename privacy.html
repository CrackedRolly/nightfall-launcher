Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what Nightfall Launcher ("the launcher") does with your information. Short version: almost nothing leaves your computer, and nothing goes to us.

Signing in

The launcher uses Microsoft's official OAuth sign-in (public client flow). You enter your credentials on Microsoft's own page. We never see, collect or store your password.

What is stored, and where

  • Access and refresh tokens, your Minecraft username, UUID and skin are stored only on your own computer, so you stay signed in.
  • Launcher settings, instances and installed mods are stored locally.
  • Signing out or uninstalling deletes the stored tokens.

What we do not do

  • We run no Nightfall servers that receive your personal data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use analytics or tracking.

Third-party services

To work, the launcher talks directly to these services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft and Xbox Live — sign-in and authentication (Microsoft Privacy Statement)
  • Mojang / Minecraft services — profile, game ownership check and game files
  • Modrinth API — searching and downloading mods (Modrinth Privacy Policy)

Children

The launcher does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children. Microsoft's own account rules apply to sign-in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue or reach out on GitHub.
