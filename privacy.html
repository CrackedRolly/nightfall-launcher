Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary in plain language

  • Nightfall has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your personal data from the launcher.
  • You sign in with Microsoft's own sign-in page. We never see your password. Your sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI) where available.
  • The launcher talks directly to Microsoft, Xbox, Mojang, Modrinth, CurseForge (only if you add your own key), mod-loader servers, GitHub (for updates) and a few others listed below. Those services see your IP address and requests, under their own privacy policies.
  • Crash logs are uploaded to mclo.gs only when you click Share, after we strip tokens from them.
  • Our Discord server and its support bot store a small amount of Discord data (like warnings, tickets, levels and birthdays you choose to save). That's explained in full below.
  • The website stores only your language choice in your browser. No cookies, no trackers.

1. Who we are

Nightfall Launcher ("Nightfall", "we", "us") is a free, small, independent project. It is not a company and it is not a registered legal entity. It is maintained by the project's developer, who is the person responsible for the processing described in this policy. The fastest way to reach us is our Discord server or GitHub issues.

Nightfall is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers three things: the Nightfall Launcher desktop app for Windows (including the Nightfall Companion mod it can install), the Nightfall website at crackedrolly.github.io/nightfall-launcher, and the official Nightfall Discord server together with its support bot. It does not cover third-party services you reach through Nightfall. Those have their own policies, linked below.

3. What we do not collect

We want to be clear about this, because it is the most important part:

  • The launcher has no telemetry, no analytics, no usage statistics, no ads and no tracking SDKs.
  • We do not operate any server that receives your Microsoft password, tokens, files, mod lists, worlds, screenshots, hardware details or play time.
  • We never see or store your Microsoft password. You type it only on Microsoft's own page.
  • We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, date of birth or payment details. Nightfall is free and has nothing to pay for.
  • We do not build profiles about you and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Signing in with Microsoft

To play Minecraft: Java Edition online you need a Microsoft account that owns the game. Nightfall uses Microsoft's official OAuth sign-in flow for public clients, with the scopes XboxLive.signin and offline_access. Here is what happens, step by step:

  1. Microsoft's sign-in page opens. You enter your details there, not in Nightfall.
  2. Microsoft gives the launcher an access token and a refresh token.
  3. The launcher exchanges them with Xbox Live (user.auth.xboxlive.com, xsts.auth.xboxlive.com) and Minecraft services (api.minecraftservices.com) to get a Minecraft token.
  4. The launcher checks that your account owns Minecraft (entitlements) and reads your Minecraft profile: username, UUID and skin/cape.

The tokens and profile are kept only on your computer (see section 5). They are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox and Mojang services, and to the Minecraft game you launch, which needs them to join servers. Microsoft's handling of your account is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Skins. If you change your skin from the launcher, the skin image you pick is uploaded to Minecraft services to change your official skin, because that is how skins work. Skins you save to your library stay in a local folder.

5. What is stored on your computer

Nightfall keeps its data in %APPDATA%\NightfallLauncher on your PC. Nothing here is sent to us.

  • Accounts: the access/refresh tokens, username, UUID and skin info for each signed-in account, stored in one file (accounts.bin) encrypted with Windows' data protection (DPAPI) through Electron's safeStorage. If the system cannot encrypt it, the file is stored unencrypted, protected only by your Windows user account.
  • Settings: your choices, such as theme, language, Simple/Kids or Advanced mode, memory, Java and performance options, and (if you add one) your CurseForge API key or Discord application id.
  • Instances (modpacks): the game files, mods, resource packs, shader packs, worlds, screenshots, options and server lists for each one.
  • Shared settings and keybinds: when you close the game, keys you changed are saved locally and copied into other instances before they launch, so your controls follow you. This happens only on your PC.
  • Skin library, cached images, downloaded Java runtimes and game libraries.
  • Logs: launcher and game logs, used for crash help. They can contain your username, file paths and hardware details. They stay on your PC unless you choose to share them.

Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder, so your worlds are safe; delete it yourself to remove everything. Signing out of an account removes its tokens.

6. Who the launcher connects to, and why

Every network request goes directly from your computer to the service below. Each one necessarily sees your IP address, the time and what was requested, plus a User-Agent naming Nightfall. We do not receive copies.

  • Microsoft / Xbox Live (login.microsoftonline.com, *.auth.xboxlive.com): sign-in. Policy.
  • Mojang / Minecraft (api.minecraftservices.com, piston-meta.mojang.com, launchermeta.mojang.com, libraries.minecraft.net, resources.download.minecraft.net, launchercontent.mojang.com): ownership check, profile, skins, game versions, game files, Java runtimes and patch notes.
  • Modrinth (api.modrinth.com and its CDN): searching and downloading mods, packs and shaders, and recommended packs. Policy.
  • CurseForge (api.curseforge.com): only if you add your own free API key. Your key is sent with each request. Overwolf/CurseForge policy.
  • Mod loaders: Fabric (meta.fabricmc.net), Quilt (meta.quiltmc.org), Forge (files/maven.minecraftforge.net) and NeoForge (maven.neoforged.net), to install the loader you pick.
  • GitHub (github.com and its download servers): automatic update checks and downloads from our public releases, plus the news and promo file on this website. Policy.
  • mc-heads.net: shows skin heads/avatars, using your public Minecraft username or UUID.
  • mclo.gs: only when you click Share on a crash (section 7).
  • Minecraft servers you add or join: the launcher may ping them to show player counts and latency, and the game connects to them when you play. Each server has its own rules.
  • Links you click (Discord invite, GPU driver pages, Microsoft redistributables, our site) open in your browser.

7. Crash help and log sharing

When the game crashes, Nightfall reads the log locally and matches it against a list of known problems to suggest a fix. That analysis does not leave your PC. If you click Share, the log is uploaded to mclo.gs, a public log-paste service, and you get a link. Before uploading, Nightfall removes access tokens; mclo.gs also strips IP addresses and some tokens on its side. Anyone with the link can read the log, so don't share it if you are not comfortable with that. mclo.gs keeps pastes under its own policy.

8. Importing from other launchers

The import tool reads folders of other launchers installed on your PC (for example CurseForge, Modrinth App, Prism, MultiMC, ATLauncher, GDLauncher, FTB App, the official launcher, Lunar, Feather, Badlion, LabyMod and others) and copies modpacks, worlds, resource packs, screenshots, server lists and keybinds into Nightfall. It copies by default and does not delete the originals. Everything happens on your PC; nothing is uploaded. Importing a CurseForge pack may download its mods from CurseForge, which needs your own API key.

9. The Nightfall Companion mod

Nightfall can add its own small mod to instances for the in-game menu, HUD, waypoints, mod toggles, customization and performance features. It stores its settings and waypoints in the instance folder on your PC. It has no telemetry and does not send data to us.

10. Discord Rich Presence

If enabled with a Discord application id, the launcher tells the Discord app running on your PC (over a local connection) what to show as your status, such as "Playing Minecraft 1.21.1". Discord then shows that to your friends, under Discord's Privacy Policy. It is off unless configured, and you can turn it off at any time.

11. Developer features

The launcher contains a Developer section used by the developer for testing. It is locked by a code that is checked on your PC against a stored hash, and it never shows in Kids mode. It collects nothing.

12. Our Discord server and support bot

When you join our Discord server, Discord itself processes your data under its own policy. Our support bot ("Nightfall Support") runs on a server the developer controls and stores, in a small database, only what its features need:

  • Moderation: warnings (your Discord user id, the reason and who issued it), timeouts, bans and the automod log.
  • Server logs: edits and deletions of messages are posted to a staff-only log channel, including the old message text.
  • Tickets and modmail: ticket messages and, when a ticket closes, a transcript of the conversation (text and attachment links) saved to a staff-only channel.
  • Bug reports and suggestions you submit, including any log you paste.
  • Levels: an XP count per user based on chatting.
  • Birthdays: only month and day, only if you use /birthday set; /birthday remove deletes it.
  • Other features you use: AFK status, reminders, applications, polls, giveaways, starboard, invite counts and temporary voice channels.
  • Crash help: /crash and log posts are analysed in memory. If a log is long, the bot may upload it to mclo.gs (tokens removed) and post the link. The bot does not keep a separate copy of logs.
  • Server backups: staff can save backups of the server's setup (channels, roles, settings), not member messages.

The bot reads messages in the server (Discord's "message content" permission) to run automod, crash detection, levels and logs. It does not sell, share or analyse this data for anything else.

13. This website, cookies and local storage

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or trackers. Fonts are served from this site, not Google. It stores one item in your browser's local storage, nf-lang, to remember your language. The download button asks the GitHub API for the latest release. GitHub, as host, may log your IP address for security and operations under its privacy statement.

14. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Data reaches third parties only as described above (because you used a feature that needs them) or if we are legally required to.

15. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where EU/EEA law, including Romania's, applies: the launcher's processing happens on your device to provide what you asked for (performance of a service you request). Shared logs are uploaded on your consent, which you give by clicking Share. Discord moderation, logs and tickets rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and helping people. Optional bot features such as birthdays rely on your consent, which you can withdraw by removing the data.

16. How long data is kept

  • Launcher data: on your PC until you delete it, sign out or uninstall.
  • Discord bot data: while the server exists or until deleted on request. Warnings and moderation records may be kept as long as needed for safety. Ticket transcripts are kept in a staff-only channel until removed.
  • mclo.gs logs: according to mclo.gs.
  • Data held by Microsoft, Mojang, Modrinth, CurseForge, GitHub or Discord: under their own rules.

17. Deleting your data

  • Sign out of an account in the launcher to delete its tokens.
  • Delete %APPDATA%\NightfallLauncher to remove everything the launcher stored (back up worlds first).
  • Use /birthday remove in Discord, or ask staff in a ticket to delete your bot data such as XP, tickets or warnings where we are allowed to.
  • To revoke Nightfall's access to your Microsoft account, visit Microsoft's account page for apps and services.

18. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent. Since the launcher keeps data only on your PC, you already have full control of it. For Discord bot data, contact us as in section 23 and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Romania that is the ANSPDCP.

19. Children's privacy

Nightfall has a Kids mode with a simpler interface. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the launcher collects none from anyone. Microsoft accounts for children are managed by Microsoft's family settings, which may require a parent's consent and can limit online play. In the EU the age of digital consent varies (16 in Romania); in the US, COPPA covers children under 13. Children under those ages should use our Discord server only if Discord's own terms allow it (at least 13) and with a parent's knowledge. If you are a parent and think your child gave us personal data through Discord, contact us and we will delete it.

20. Security

Tokens are encrypted with Windows DPAPI where available. Access tokens are removed from logs before sharing. Updates are downloaded only from our official GitHub releases and checked against the release's file hash before installing. No method is perfect: keep Windows updated, don't share your logs or %APPDATA% folder publicly, and only download Nightfall from our website or GitHub. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.

21. International users

The launcher works on your device, wherever you are. Third-party services and the Discord bot's host may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

22. Changes to this policy

If the launcher starts doing something new with data, we will update this page before or when it ships, change the date at the top and mention it in the changelog. Older versions remain in the site's public GitHub history.

23. Contact

Ask in our Discord server (open a ticket for private questions) or on GitHub. Please don't post tokens or private information publicly.
